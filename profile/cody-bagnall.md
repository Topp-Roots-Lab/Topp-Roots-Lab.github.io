## Research interests / expertise

Agricultural systems, Remote sensing, Proximal sensing, and Machine development.

## Bio

G. Cody Bagnall is a research scientist in the Topp lab at the Donald Danforth Plant Science Center. He received his B.S. degree in Aerospace Engineering from Texas A&M University in 2010, and his M.S. and PhD degrees in Biological and Agricultural Engineering from Texas A&M University in 2014 and 2020, respectively. His current research interests include remote and proximal sensing in agricultural systems and sensor systems development for plant phenotyping with a focus on root phenotyping systems.
