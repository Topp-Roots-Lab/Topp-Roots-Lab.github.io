## Education

- Ph.D. in Soil Science, Washington State University
- B.A. in Chemistry, Michigan State University

## Research Interests

Katherine’s research focuses on soil health, with an emphasis on understanding soil carbon dynamics and ecosystem functioning across diverse agricultural and natural ecosystems. Using interdisciplinary approaches, she investigates how cropping systems, land management practices, and environmental conditions influence soil properties, belowground processes, and the ecosystem services that sustain productivity and environmental resilience. Her graduate and postdoctoral research emphasized soil profile health assessments across space and time, with particular attention to soil biological health and multi-omics. Her work has involved collaborations with agricultural producers and researchers from the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service, USDA Agricultural Research Service, and U.S. Department of Energy to advance soil health evaluation and ecosystem service monitoring.
